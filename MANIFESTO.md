# The Weave Manifesto

**Weave is a tool for thinking through hard things** — a reasoning graph where
a problem decomposes into ideas, ideas are answered or blocked, and the whole
shape of your thinking becomes something you can see, question, and revise.

It is built on five promises.

### 1. Your work is yours

Your thinking is stored on your own device, and every project exports to a plain,
readable file you can keep, move, back up, or open anywhere. Nothing is locked in
a format only Weave can read. If Weave disappeared tomorrow, your thinking would
still be yours, in a format that outlives any app.

*(In Chrome and Edge you can point Weave at a folder on your disk and it writes
your work there as you go — data plus readable Markdown. Elsewhere it stays in
your browser's local store, on your machine, until those browsers catch up.)*

### 2. Your key, your AI

Weave's AI runs on **your** API key, from the provider **you** choose. The key
lives on your device and is used to talk to your AI directly. Connect one and
the AI helps you decompose, constrain, and stress-test your ideas. Connect
nothing and Weave works exactly as well — just without the AI. There is no
Weave account standing between you and your model.

### 3. We never see your graph

There is no Weave server holding your reasoning. Nothing you write is uploaded
to us, sold, mined, or read. The most private thing about a thinking tool is
the thinking — and here it stays on your machine, under your control. That
isn't a feature we added. It's the shape of the thing.

### 4. Open, and yours to run

Weave is open source under the **AGPL-3.0** license. Read it, fork it, and run
your own copy — a hosted instance you deploy in one click, or a local build on
your laptop. It is yours to inspect and yours to keep running, on your terms.

### 5. Free at its core — forever

The full Weave is free. Not a trial, not a crippled "lite" version — the whole
tool. That will not change.

### Supporting Weave

Weave is designed, built, and cared for by one person, in the hours found
around everything else. It is free, and it will stay free.

If it comes to matter in how you think, you're warmly invited to support its
making — [buy me a coffee ☕](https://buymeacoffee.com/adhiet1810). What you give goes
toward the time and craft behind Weave, and toward where it grows from here. It
is an invitation, offered gently and gratefully — never an obligation, and
never a gate placed in front of the tool.

*(In time, an optional convenience — private, encrypted sync across your own
devices — may be offered to those who'd like it. If it ever arrives, it will
hold only what cannot be read, and the free, self-hosted Weave will always work
fully without it.)*
